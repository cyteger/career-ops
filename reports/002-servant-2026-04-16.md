## ⚠️ Upfront caveat — Part-time structure

The JD is explicit: **"20-40 hours per week"** with a **"fixed monthly stipend"**. This is a **part-time contractor** position, not full-time employment. Before applying, decide:

- **Does this fit alongside your GTBank day job?** 20hrs/wk on top of a full-time Team Lead role is sustainable only if you're ready for a ~60hr/wk life. 40hrs/wk means quitting GTBank for a part-time stipend — unlikely to meet your $70K annual target unless the stipend is unusually generous.
- **Is the stipend in USD or GHS?** Ghana-based contract roles often pay in local currency, which compresses against your international-remote target range.
- **Does Servant disclose the stipend amount before applying?** If not, clarify in the first screening call. You want concrete numbers before investing interview time.

If this is a side contract you want for Azure exposure + mission alignment, the fit is strong. If this is meant as primary income, caution.
