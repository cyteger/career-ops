- Strong grasp of prompt engineering techniques (CoT, RAG, few-shot)
- Proven ability to train, coach, or upskill non-technical staff on AI tools
- Excellent communication, facilitation, and presentation skills
- Proficiency in Python or scripting for demonstrations and automation
- AI/ML certification or formal training is a plus
